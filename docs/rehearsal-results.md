## Independent audit findings

- **Stock reach / liquid contact:** the configured 15 mL wells are 117.5 mm deep. Aspiration at 1.5 mm needs 116 mm insertion, versus ~51.8 mm attached p300 tip reach and ~80.1 mm p1000 reach. Live preparation/export now rejects it even if release flags are checked. Actual suitable short-container/rack geometry is required; no physical definition was fabricated. This proves nozzle entry, not certain mechanical collision. The check conservatively excludes it until appropriate geometry is supplied; SDK simulation does not validate nozzle clearance or immersion.
- **Calibration:** an R² of 0.999877 could conceal a 10 µM standard back-calculating to 3.41 µM while the high independent check passed. Every nonzero fit level now must recover within 15% (20% at the lowest level). Quantification cannot extend below the greater of measured LOQ and the lowest nonzero calibrator.
- **Paid retries:** the staff helper previously refused an existing output folder, encouraging a new campaign. Unchanged inputs now resume the same hashed ingestion/scoring records; altered archives stop, accepted scores are reused and uncertain API attempts remain blocked.
- **Reaction hold:** preparation is checked between actions/strokes and stops if the hold expires. Actual elapsed/overrun times are logged; excessive final overrun fails. An external operator timer is still essential because a blocking App pause prevents automatic module shutdown.
- **Preparation time:** the former 60-minute allowance was below programmed pumping/waits alone. Manifests expose that lower bound, excluding motion and staff pauses; recalculate it after changing stock sources. The replacement 120-minute scenario remains unmeasured, and no 12-hour completion is demonstrated. Analytical charges start at 5 mg, including separately weighed liquid product FIT/CHECK charges.

The [official GEN2 dimensions, p. 13](https://insights.opentrons.com/hubfs/Products/Pipettes/GEN2%20Pipette%20White%20Paper.pdf) show exposed shafts that add reach beyond the disposable tips; the original inference of certain physical inaccessibility was therefore rejected. The concrete concern is the ~9 mL analytical IS master aspirated by P300 near its bottom: an idealized liquid-height estimate from the [Corning tube drawing](https://www.corning.com/catalog/cls/documents/drawings/LSR00039-Falcon-Conical-Tube-15mL-PP-352096-352097-and-352196.pdf) suggests nozzle immersion. This is an inference to check, not a measured collision. The conservative policy blocks nozzle entry until the source/aspiration approach is redesigned; compatible shorter sources are one route.
